## Basic Usage

Input: (Image, i OR Video, v) AND PromptText, p
Output: Masks, m AND Bboxs, bbox 


## SAM3 - Promptable Concept Segmentation (PCS) for Images

Input: (Image, i  AND (PromptText, pt OR PromptBBox, pBbox)) OR ((Video, v  AND PromptText, pt))
Output: Masks, m
